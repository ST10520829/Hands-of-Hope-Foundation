A quick note

What happens next?

Will this form email the foundation?

No. This is a front-end assessment demonstration. It validates the entries and displays a review summary locally; it does not send or save them.

Can I make a real donation here?

No. Hands of Hope is fictional and this website does not accept payments. The donation page only demonstrates responsible enquiry design.
